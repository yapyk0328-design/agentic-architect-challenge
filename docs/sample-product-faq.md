# Acme Cloud Storage FAQ (sample document)

Acme Cloud Storage offers a Starter plan with 10 GB of storage for $5 per month and a Pro plan with 100 GB for $12 per month. Support is available Monday through Friday, 9:00–17:00 Malaysia time. To reset a password, select "Forgot password" on the sign-in page and follow the email link. This sample document does not specify a refund policy.
